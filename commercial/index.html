Restaurant Services

Restaurant and Food Service HVAC

Restaurants and food-service businesses have climate demands that ordinary equipment can't meet. Kitchens generate enormous heat loads while dining rooms need to stay comfortable, exhaust hoods pull out conditioned air that makeup air units must replace, and a busy Friday night pushes rooftop units to their limit. Gresham HVAC specializes in food-service HVAC, including rooftop package units, split systems, correct tonnage and airflow sizing, makeup air balancing against hood exhaust, and scheduled service that works around your hours.

Comfort and air balance are critical concerns for any food-service operator. A kitchen running negative pressure pulls dining-room air the wrong way, drags outdoor air through every door, and makes staff miserable during peak service. Our work keeps your system delivering the temperatures and airflow your operation depends on, with documented service records that demonstrate a well-maintained facility to landlords and auditors. We provide detailed reports and service logs after each visit, along with the documentation many commercial leases require.

We work directly with owners, managers, and kitchen staff to plan replacements and maintenance that protect your business and fit your operating schedule. Our technicians are used to working in occupied facilities, installing during off-hours when needed, minimizing disruption, and keeping your kitchen running and your dining room comfortable.

Inspections & Documentation

Safe Installations, Documented Service

Safety is part of every commercial job we do. Our technicians inspect heat exchangers for cracks, confirm correct venting and combustion air, check electrical connections and disconnects, verify the refrigerant charge, clear condensate lines, and check ventilation rates in occupied areas. The goal is simple: protecting the people in your building from CO hazards, poor air quality, and water damage. You receive a written report that ranks each issue by priority and recommends a specific fix.

The paperwork matters as much as the repair. Every account gets a documentation binder with detailed service logs and temperature and filter records, giving you proof of due diligence for landlords, insurers, and auditors. Heavy-use equipment, in kitchens, gyms, and large mechanical rooms, gets a more frequent visit schedule.

Rapid Response

Fast Commercial HVAC Service

Each hour without climate control costs your business money. In a restaurant, it can shut down the kitchen. On a retail floor, it empties the aisles on a summer afternoon. In a server room, it means overheated equipment, and in a leased building it invites tenant disputes. Gresham HVAC offers fast emergency HVAC service for commercial properties throughout Gresham and surrounding areas, with an experienced technician dispatched as soon as you call.

No two commercial emergencies look the same. The cause could be a failed compressor, a burned-out condenser fan motor, a refrigerant leak, an ignition or gas valve fault, a tripped high-limit switch, a frozen coil, or a control board stuck in lockout. Whatever it is, we diagnose it fast, get the building comfortable again, and document the entire call for your facility files. Then we put maintenance and monitoring in place so the same failure doesn't return.
